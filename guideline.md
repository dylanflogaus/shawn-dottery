# Campaign Website Requirements

## Project
Campaign website for **Shawn Dottery**, Republican candidate for Delaware State Representative (3rd District).

**Theme:** "A Voice for the Quiet People"

## Brand

- Clean, patriotic, modern design
- Red, white, and blue color palette
- Mobile-first responsive layout
- Working-class, community-focused aesthetic
- Emphasis on trust, humility, and service

## Navigation

- Home
- Meet Shawn
- Priorities
- The Quiet People
- News / Updates
- Get Involved
- Donate
- Contact

## The Quiet People

Dedicated page explaining campaign philosophy.

Audience includes:
- Working families
- Tradespeople
- Nurses
- Teachers
- Police
- Firefighters
- Veterans
- Seniors
- Small business owners
- Parents
- Retirees

Core message:
Government should work for people who quietly work hard, pay taxes, raise families, and contribute to their communities.

## Priorities

Five issue pages:

1. Cost of Living
- Lower everyday expenses
- Responsible budgeting
- Taxpayer value

2. Safe Neighborhoods
- Public safety
- Community partnerships
- Support law enforcement

3. Education
- Strong public schools
- Career & technical education
- Apprenticeships
- Parent involvement

4. Small Business & Jobs
- Reduce unnecessary barriers
- Encourage entrepreneurship
- Support local employers

5. Government Accountability
- Transparency
- Fiscal responsibility
- Respect taxpayers
