The Quiet People

Delaware runs on people you rarely hear about.

The ones who clock in before sunrise. Who stretch a paycheck across rent, groceries, and a kid’s school shoes. Who show up for church, for youth sports, for aging parents – and still find a way to check on a neighbor.

  • They don’t hire lobbyists.
  • They don’t chase cameras.
  • They just keep the place going.

I’m Shawn Dottery. I’m running for State Representative because too many of those people have been talked past. I’m running for the nurse ending a double shift, the tradesperson watching prices climb, the senior on a fixed income, the small business owner keeping the lights on one more month.

“I see the work you do. I hear what you carry. I’m here to fight for you.”
