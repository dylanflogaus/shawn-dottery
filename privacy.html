Privacy Policy

How this campaign website handles information you share with us.

Last updated: August 3, 2026

Friends of Shawn Dottery (“the Campaign”) respects your privacy. This page explains, in plain language, how information collected through this website may be used.

Information we collect

If you join our email list, volunteer, RSVP to an event, or send a contact message, you may provide your name, email address, phone number, ZIP code, and any message you choose to include.

How we use information

We use this information to communicate about the campaign, organize volunteers and events, respond to inquiries, and improve the website experience. We do not sell your personal information.

Donations

Online donations, when enabled, will be processed through a third-party platform. That platform’s privacy policy and terms will apply to payment information.

Cookies and analytics

This site may use basic analytics or cookies in the future to understand traffic and improve performance. Details will be updated here before such tools are added.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to info@shawndottery.com.
